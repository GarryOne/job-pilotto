How it works

Four stops, from new opening to reply

Each stop runs on its own. Open one to see everything in it.

  1. 01 · FIND

    Finds the right openings and scores them

    Career pages of top tech employers, Swiss job boards and Google Jobs, every few hours. Each job gets a fit score out of 100 with the reason; the best arrive in Telegram.

    Everything in this stop
    • Employer career pages from their public job feeds (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters and more)
    • Swiss job boards (jobs.ch, TechTree), optional Google Jobs, and a daily scout that adds new employers
    • Filters by city, remote and languages you don't speak; jobs you applied to or dismissed never come back
    • Facts from each posting (languages, seniority, salary, visa), each with a quote as proof; visa badges
    • Edit your profile and every open job is scored again
  2. 02 · PREPARE

    Drafts your application

    One tap: an answer for every question on the employer's real form, a cover letter in your voice, and a CV tailored to the job.

    Everything in this stop
    • An application kit per job, with an eligibility check ("not eligible" and why)
    • Tailored CV: bullets reordered and reworded toward the posting; numbers, titles and dates can't change; every change highlighted
    • Sounds like you: drafts start from your CV and answers you've written; anything uncertain is flagged, never guessed
    • Answer once: questions it couldn't answer are asked once and reused on every later form
  3. 03 · APPLY

    Fills the form. You submit.

    The Chrome extension fills a form in seconds. With Claude Code, Apply with Claude also gets through the job board, the employer's site and its sign-up to the form. Where your click happens

    Everything in this stop
    • Fill in Chrome: text fields, dropdowns, phone, city search, checkbox questions, CV and cover letter; a live checklist of what's left
    • Answers written by AI are outlined so you read them first; consent boxes stay yours unless you allow it
    • Apply with Claude: follows Apply to the employer's site, creates the account if asked (password generated into your Keychain, never seen), reads the confirmation email (Gmail, read-only)
    • On a form the extension knows, Claude hands it to the extension and fills only what's left
    • Marks the job Applied when the confirmation page appears
  4. 04 · TRACK

    Replies, interviews and what to change New: interviews

    Gmail and Calendar update each application by themselves. Record an interview and get a transcript with who said what, filed under the job, plus a review if you want one.

    Everything in this stop
    • Gmail and Calendar (read-only): confirmations, replies, interviews and rejections; a prep message the evening before
    • Interviews: record the call or add a recording; transcribed on your Mac, free, speakers told apart, saved to Notion under the job
    • An interview review in one click (about $0.05): every question, how you answered, what to practise
    • A daily insight, a weekly report and your funnel, with the step to improve
    • Telegram for matches and commands; Notion with its views and mobile app; runs while your Mac is off with your free GitHub

    Ask first, every time. The app won't record until you confirm everyone on the call agreed. Recording people without their consent is illegal in Switzerland and many other places.

FAQ

Questions before take-off

Does it apply to jobs for me?

No, never. It finds, scores, drafts and fills. Submitting is always your click, after you've reviewed the form.

Do I need Notion?

Yes, a free Notion account. It's where you see and track everything: matches, applications, interviews and insights. The app copies a ready-made workspace into your account during setup.

What does the AI cost?

You use your own Anthropic account, so the cost goes to you directly and you can see it: usually a few dollars a month, capped by the monthly budget you set. Each run shows what it cost in your Notion.

How is it different from Simplify, Teal, Huntr or JobCopilot?

It does the whole search, not one step: finds and scores jobs, drafts each form's answers, tailors your CV, fills the form and tracks replies from your Gmail, with your data in your own Notion. It never mass-applies. See the side-by-side comparison.

Windows or Linux?

Mac first. Windows is planned once the Mac app is stable.

Which jobs does it cover?

Today it focuses on tech roles in Switzerland where English is enough, plus remote roles open to people in Switzerland. It reads career pages of top tech employers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Stripe, Datadog, Cloudflare and more), jobs.ch, TechTree and Google Jobs.

Does it work with LinkedIn?

It doesn't read or post on LinkedIn. Most roles you'd find there are also on the employer's own career page, which is where it looks and where you apply.

What happens to my CV?

It stays on your Mac. The AI reads it to draft your Profile (which you review in Notion) and, when you ask, to make a version tailored to one job; you see every change before it's used. Nothing is stored on a Job Pilotto server.

Can I run it myself for free?

Yes. Job Pilotto is open source: run it from the source code with the same Notion workspace. The Mac app is free too. Pro, coming soon, adds hosted searches and the maintained employer index.
