How it works

Four legs, from new opening to reply

Every step runs on its own. The only thing it waits for is you pressing Submit.

FIND · every 4 h

Finds the right openings

Career pages of top tech employers, Swiss job boards and Google Jobs. Roles that need German or French you don't speak are filtered out.

SCORE · per job

Scores them against your CV

A fit score out of 100 with the reasons, quoted from the posting. Your best matches arrive in Telegram.

PREPARE · 1 tap

Drafts your application

A cover letter and an answer for every form question, written in your voice from your CV and past answers.

APPLY · you submit

Fills the form, tracks replies

Pick how many jobs to apply to; each form is filled for you. You check it and submit. Replies and interviews land in your Notion.

FAQ

Questions before take-off

Does it apply to jobs for me?

No, never. It finds, scores, drafts and fills. Submitting is always your click, after you've reviewed the form.

Do I need Notion?

Yes, a free Notion account. It's where you see and track everything: matches, applications, interviews and insights. The app copies a ready-made workspace into your account during setup.

What does the AI cost?

You use your own Anthropic account, so the cost goes to you directly and you can see it: usually a few dollars a month. Each search shows what it cost in your Notion.

Windows or Linux?

Mac first. Windows is planned once the pilot is running well.

Which jobs does it cover?

The pilot focuses on tech roles in Switzerland where English is enough, plus remote roles open to people in Switzerland. It reads career pages of top tech employers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Stripe, Datadog, Cloudflare and more), jobs.ch, TechTree and Google Jobs.

Does it work with LinkedIn?

It doesn't read or post on LinkedIn. Most roles you'd find there are also on the employer's own career page, which is where it looks and where you apply.

What happens to my CV?

It stays on your Mac. The AI reads it once to draft your Profile (which you review in Notion), and it's attached to the applications you submit. Nothing is stored on a Job Pilotto server.

Can I run it myself for free?

Yes. Job Pilotto is open source: run it from the source code with the same Notion workspace. Pro is the signed app with guided setup, updates and the maintained list of employer pages.
