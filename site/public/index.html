How it works

Four legs, from new opening to reply

Every step runs on its own. The only thing it waits for is you pressing Submit.

FIND · every 4 h

Finds the right openings

Career pages of top tech employers, Swiss job boards and Google Jobs. Roles that need German or French you don't speak are filtered out.

SCORE · per job

Scores them against your CV

A fit score out of 100 with the reasons, quoted from the posting. Your best matches arrive in Telegram.

PREPARE · 1 tap

Drafts your application

A cover letter and an answer for every form question, written in your voice from your CV and past answers.

APPLY · you submit

Fills the form, tracks replies

Pick how many jobs to apply to; each form is filled for you. You check it and submit. Replies and interviews land in your Notion.

Features

Everything it does, from search to reply

All of it is in the free Mac app. Optional parts switch on when you connect them (Telegram, Google, GitHub).

FIND

New openings, every few hours

  • Employer career pages of top tech companies, read from their public job feeds (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters and more)
  • Swiss job boards (jobs.ch, TechTree) and optional Google Jobs
  • A daily scout that finds new employers worth watching and adds them
  • Filters by city, remote, and languages you don't speak; jobs you applied to or dismissed never come back
SCORE

Each job read and scored for you

  • Facts from every posting: languages, seniority, work mode, salary, visa, each with a quote as proof
  • A fit score out of 100 against your profile, with the reason in one line
  • Visa badges when you'd need sponsorship, or when the job offers it
  • Edit your profile and every open job is scored again
PREPARE

Your application, drafted

  • An application kit per job: reads the employer's real form, drafts an answer for every question and a cover letter in your voice
  • An eligibility check in the kit: "not eligible" with the reason when a job clearly rules you out
  • Answer once: questions it couldn't answer are listed once in the app and reused on every later form
  • Anything uncertain is flagged for you to check, never guessed
TAILOR New

A CV tailored to each job

  • One click per job: bullets reordered and reworded toward the posting, the summary rewritten for it
  • Only your own facts: numbers can't change, new tools or claims are flagged, titles and dates stay as they are
  • See every change: reworded words, moved and dropped bullets highlighted, each with the reason
  • A clean default design or your own, and the extension uploads the tailored CV on that job's form
FILL · CHROME

Forms filled in seconds

  • Press Fill in Chrome (or Apply without Claude Code): Chrome opens the form and the extension fills it from your kit
  • Text fields, dropdowns, phone country, city search boxes, checkbox questions, CV and cover letter
  • A live checklist on the page of what's left, updated as you go, turning green when it's ready to submit
  • Answers written by AI are outlined so you read them first; consent boxes are ticked only if you allow it
  • Follows a job board's Apply to the employer's own site, and works on any site with one click
  • Marks the job Applied by itself when the confirmation page appears
APPLY · CLAUDE New

Apply with Claude, all the way to the form

  • Recommended when you have Claude Code: one button, and Claude drives your Chrome from the posting to the last form page
  • Follows a job board's Apply to the employer's site and its own Apply now, across every page
  • Creates the employer account if the site asks, with a password generated into your Mac's Keychain that it never sees
  • Reads the confirmation email from your Gmail (read-only) and confirms the account itself
  • Asks you only for the CAPTCHA and the terms boxes, then stops before Submit
TRACK · LEARN

Replies, interviews and what to change

  • Gmail and Calendar (read-only): confirmations, replies, interviews and rejections update each application by themselves
  • A prep message the evening before an interview
  • Interviews recorded and transcribed on your Mac, with who said what, saved to Notion under the job (more)
  • Interview reviews from a transcript or your notes: every question, how you answered, what to practise
  • A daily insight, a weekly report and your funnel, with the step to improve
  • Gets better at each job site's forms with every fill
EVERYWHERE

On your phone, in your Notion

  • Telegram: your best new matches with buttons to save, prepare, dismiss or mark applied, and commands like /applied
  • Notion workspace: matches, applications, interviews, insights; with its views, filters and mobile app
  • Keeps working while your Mac is off: the app sets up your own private GitHub repository to run on your schedule
  • Notifications on your Mac: kit ready, form filled, application sent
CONTROL

You stay in charge

  • Never submits for you, and never applies in bulk
  • Your keys in your Mac's Keychain, your data on your Mac and in your Notion
  • An AI budget cap with alerts; every run and its cost logged
  • Open source: read it, run it yourself, or change it
New · Interviews

Every interview transcribed, with who said what

Record the call in the app, or add a recording you already have. It's transcribed on your Mac, free, and saved to your Notion next to the job it belongs to.

  • Speakers told apart. Each line says who spoke. When you record in the app, it knows which voice is yours.
  • Private and free. Open-source speech models run on your Mac. The recording never leaves it, and no minutes are billed.
  • Filed under the right job. Pick the application and the transcript lands in your Notion 🎤 Interviews, linked to it. You can change the job at any time.
  • A review if you want one. One click and Claude goes through every question: what landed, what a stronger answer would add, and what to practise for the next round. About $0.05.
  • From your phone too. Send a voice note or recording to your Telegram bot after the call.

Ask first, every time. Before recording, tell everyone on the call and record only if they all agree. The app won't start recording until you confirm that they did. Recording people without their consent is illegal in Switzerland and many other places.

FAQ

Questions before take-off

Does it apply to jobs for me?

No, never. It finds, scores, drafts and fills. Submitting is always your click, after you've reviewed the form.

Do I need Notion?

Yes, a free Notion account. It's where you see and track everything: matches, applications, interviews and insights. The app copies a ready-made workspace into your account during setup.

What does the AI cost?

You use your own Anthropic account, so the cost goes to you directly and you can see it: usually a few dollars a month. Each search shows what it cost in your Notion.

How is it different from Simplify, Teal, Huntr or JobCopilot?

It does the whole search, not one step: finds and scores jobs, drafts each form's answers, tailors your CV, fills the form and tracks replies from your Gmail, with your data in your own Notion. It never mass-applies. See the side-by-side comparison.

Windows or Linux?

Mac first. Windows is planned once the Mac app is stable.

Which jobs does it cover?

Today it focuses on tech roles in Switzerland where English is enough, plus remote roles open to people in Switzerland. It reads career pages of top tech employers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Stripe, Datadog, Cloudflare and more), jobs.ch, TechTree and Google Jobs.

Does it work with LinkedIn?

It doesn't read or post on LinkedIn. Most roles you'd find there are also on the employer's own career page, which is where it looks and where you apply.

What happens to my CV?

It stays on your Mac. The AI reads it to draft your Profile (which you review in Notion) and, when you ask, to make a version tailored to one job; you see every change before it's used. Nothing is stored on a Job Pilotto server.

Can I run it myself for free?

Yes. Job Pilotto is open source: run it from the source code with the same Notion workspace. The Mac app is free too. Pro, coming soon, adds hosted searches and the maintained employer index.
