Platform support

What Jobadabra runs on

Today: a Mac or Windows app, the Chrome or Edge extension, Gmail for replies, and Claude for the AI. Below is what works now, what comes next, and what we are weighing.

Works today

Supported now

Statustoday
MacApple Silicon (M1 or later), macOS✓
WindowsWindows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Beta, not signed yet, so Windows asks you to confirm the install◐ beta
Google Chromethe extension that fills application forms✓
Microsoft Edgesame extension; the app opens your forms in Edge. Arrives with the next app update◐ next update
Brave, Vivaldiother Chromium browsers: the app finds the extension there; we don't test them regularly◐
Gmail and Google Calendarread-only, optional: replies, interviews and rejections update your tracker✓
Claude, with your own keyan Anthropic API key, usually a few dollars a month✓
Claude Code, on your own planoptional: uses your Claude subscription instead of API credits, and Apply with Claude✓
Notionyour job search lives in your own workspace✓
Telegram, GitHuboptional: matches on your phone, and runs while your computer is off✓
