Where the others are the better choice

🌍

Any job, anywhere

Simplify fills forms on over 100,000 career sites and matches you to millions of openings. Job Pilotto focuses on tech roles in Switzerland and remote Europe today, and fills the big application systems best.

🚀

Volume

If you want hundreds of applications a day, JobCopilot and LazyApply do that. Job Pilotto won't: it's built for fewer, better applications that you check.

🎨

CV templates

Teal and Huntr have polished resume builders with many templates. Job Pilotto tailors the CV you already have, in its own design or a clean default.

🧩

No setup

The others are a sign-up away. Job Pilotto needs a Mac, a free Notion account and your own AI key: about ten minutes, guided by the app.
