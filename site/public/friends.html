Thanks for trying Job Pilotto 🙏

You're one of the first people outside me to use it. It finds tech jobs that fit you, drafts your answers, fills the forms and tracks everything in your own Notion. You review and click Submit. It's early: things will break, and that's exactly what I need to hear about. — Igor

  1. 1 · Install (Mac, Apple silicon)Paste in Terminal: curl -fsSL https://www.jobpilotto.workers.dev/install | bash Or download it and allow it once in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway. Windows: beta installer.
    1 min
  2. 2 · Set it upThe app walks you through it: your Anthropic key (the AI runs on your Mac with it), a Notion workspace, your CV and what you're looking for.
    10 min
  3. 3 · Unlock it (if I gave you a key)It's free for your first 30 applications or 60 days. A founder key from me removes the limit: paste it in Settings → License.
    10 s
  4. 4 · Use it for your real searchLet it find and score jobs, prepare a kit, fill one form. Go at your pace.
    2 weeks
  5. 5 · Tell me the truthIn the app: Send feedback (bottom of the sidebar), or just message me. What I'd love to know:
    • Where did you get stuck or confused?
    • What was genuinely useful?
    • Would you pay for it while searching, and how much?
    any time

🔒 Your CV, answers and applications stay on your Mac and in your own Notion; they never touch my servers. Why you can trust the download
