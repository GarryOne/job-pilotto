Early testers

Thanks for trying Jobadabra 🙏

You're one of the first people outside me to use it. It finds jobs that fit you, for any role across Europe, drafts your answers, fills the forms and tracks everything in the app. You review and click Submit. It's early: things will break, and that's exactly what I need to hear about. — Igor

  1. 1 · Install (Mac with Apple silicon, Windows 10 or 11)Paste in Terminal: curl -fsSL https://www.jobadabra.top/install | bash Or download it for Mac or Windows and allow it once (Mac: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway; Windows: More info → Run anyway).
    1 min
  2. 2 · Set it upThe app walks you through it: an AI (Free, or your own key), your CV and what you're looking for.
    An optional own key (2 min, for stronger letters): sign up at console.anthropic.com (separate from a Claude.ai subscription), add about $5 of credit under Billing (it lasts weeks; you set a monthly cap in the app), then Create Key and paste it. The AI runs on your Mac with that key.
    No Anthropic account? Pick "Free" in that step: nothing to set up. Add your own key later.
    10 min
  3. 3 · Know the free trialIt's free for 20 applications or 60 days, whichever lasts longer. Settings → Plan shows where you stand.
    10 s
  4. 4 · Use it for your real searchLet it find and score jobs, prepare a kit, fill one form. Go at your pace.
    2 weeks
  5. 5 · Tell me the truthIn the app: Send feedback (bottom of the sidebar), or just message me. What I'd love to know:
    • Where did you get stuck or confused?
    • What was genuinely useful?
    • Would you pay for it while searching, and how much?
    any time
