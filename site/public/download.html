Setup

The first ten minutes

  1. Open the appIt walks you through the rest, step by step
    1 min
  2. Pick your AIFree AI needs nothing; or paste your own Claude or ChatGPT key for stronger letters
    1 min
  3. Choose your CV, say what you wantThe roles and places you're after; the AI drafts your strategy for you to review
    5 min
  4. Add the browser extensionChrome or Edge, so forms can be filled for you
    1 min
  5. OptionalGmail for replies, Telegram on your phone, Notion for your tracker, GitHub to run while your computer is off (all platforms)
    + 5 min
For technical users: install on a Mac from Terminal

No security prompt this way. Paste in Terminal:

curl -fsSL https://www.jobadabra.top/install | bash

Linux, or any computer: run it from the source.
