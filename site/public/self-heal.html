Self-healing · AI spend · 2–3 Oct 2026

The self-healing loops cost about $4–11 in AI in their first two days

The Finder reviews the app's screenshots and files issues. The Fixer turns an issue into a pull request. Both run on Claude Sonnet 5.5, and the Finder's screenshot review is most of the bill.

What drives the cost

  • Volume of screenshot reviews. There were 476 of them, about one per screenshot in every e2e suite, four e2e runs a day.
  • Thinking tokens. Sonnet 5.5 thinks by default, and thinking counts as output ($10 per million tokens, up to the 1,200-token cap). That is roughly half to two-thirds of each review.
  • No prompt caching. Every review resends the prompt and the design rules, and the screenshot comes first, so nothing after it can be cached.
  • The Fixer is cheap per attempt ($0.17–0.25, 14–22 turns). Its cost is in the attempts that end in a closed PR: 2 of 4.
