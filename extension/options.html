Job Pilotto settings

The extension talks only to the Job Pilotto app on this Mac. Your details and CV stay in the app.

Connection

Both are filled in by Connect. Keep the Job Pilotto app open while you apply.

Filling

On: Job Pilotto doesn't fill a form when the job clearly excludes you (location, work permit, language), and tells you why; you can still choose Fill anyway. Turn it off to always fill, e.g. while testing.
For trying out the filling on real forms without submitting: Claude answers every field and never stops for eligibility. Turn it off for real applications.
Some sites (e.g. Greenhouse) only open their drop-down menus for a real click. With this on, the extension clicks them for you; Chrome shows "Job Pilotto started debugging this browser" while it does.
Privacy notices, "I acknowledge", data-processing consents: ticked after filling (listed on the page so you can read them), and Submit is left ready for you. Job Pilotto never presses Submit.

Your details and CV

They live in the Job Pilotto app (Settings → Your details for application forms), and the extension asks the app for them each time it fills a form. Change them there.
