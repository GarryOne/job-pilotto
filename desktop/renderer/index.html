Check-in · about 5 minutes

Let's set up your job search

Job Pilotto finds openings that fit you, scores them against your CV, and helps you fill applications. You review and click Submit yourself.

  • Add your AI key (Anthropic)
  • Set up your Job Pilotto workspace in Notion (free)
  • Choose your CV
  • Say what you're looking for
  • Review the strategy it drafts for you
Step 1 of 6

Connect your AI

Job Pilotto uses Claude to read job postings, score them and draft answers. You pay Anthropic directly, usually a few dollars a month.

  1. Open console.anthropic.com → API keys and sign in.
  2. Click Create Key, name it "Job Pilotto", copy it.
  3. Paste it here.

Step 2 of 6

Your Job Pilotto workspace in Notion

Notion is where you'll see and track everything: your matches, applications, replies, interviews and insights. A free Notion account is enough.

Notion opens in your browser. Choose Use a template (Notion copies the Job Pilotto workspace into your Notion), or pick an empty page named Job Pilotto: Job Pilotto builds everything inside it. Then come back here.

Paste a token instead
  1. Make a page for it: in Notion's sidebar, + (new page) → name it Job Pilotto and leave it empty. Job Pilotto builds everything inside it when you connect. (Or copy → Duplicate; missing parts are added the same way.)
  2. Create a connection: open Notion's Developer tools → Connections → New connection → name it "Job Pilotto", keep API token → Create connection → copy the token (starts with ntn_).
  3. Give it access: on the connection's Content access tab → Edit access → under Private → Top level pages, tick only Job Pilotto → Save.
  4. Paste the token here.

      Your data
      
        
      
        
      Export saves this computer's Job Pilotto data in one file (settings, CV and tailored CVs, recordings,
          interview drafts, job list, run history), to keep or move to another computer. Import replaces the data here with such a file
          (the current data is kept as a backup). Your Notion workspace is never in the file: it stays in Notion.
      
        
        
      
        
      
      
      
      
      
        
      ⚠️ Danger zone: reset Job Pilotto on this computer
      
          
      Removed from this computer: your AI, Notion, Telegram and GitHub keys, your CV and tailored CVs, call recordings and
            interview drafts, the job list, schedule and settings. Job Pilotto restarts at the setup, like the first time.
      
          
      Not changed: your Notion workspace and everything in it, your Gmail sign-in, your GitHub repo. The Chrome extension
            has to be connected again.
