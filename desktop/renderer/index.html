Check-in · about 5 minutes

Let's set up your job search

Job Pilotto finds openings that fit you, scores them against your CV, and helps you fill applications. You review and click Submit yourself.

  • Add your AI key (Anthropic)
  • Set up your Job Pilotto workspace in Notion (free)
  • Choose your CV
  • Check the goals and strategy it proposes from your CV

Job Pilotto sends technical reports (errors, forms it couldn't fill, anonymous counts like forms filled and interviews) to improve it for everyone, and shares which employer career pages you use, with two labels (your kind of role and your region), to grow the shared pool of employers. Never your answers, CV, emails or names. Turn either off any time in Settings → Advanced.

Step 1 of 5

Connect your AI

Job Pilotto uses Claude to read job postings, score them and draft answers. Choose how it runs: your own Claude Code (your Claude plan), or an Anthropic API key (you pay Anthropic per use, usually a few dollars a month).

  1. Open console.anthropic.com → API keys and sign up or sign in (a separate account from a Claude.ai subscription).
  2. New account? Go to Billing and add a small amount of credit, e.g. $5: it lasts weeks. Keys don't work without credit.
  3. Back in API keys: click Create Key, name it "Job Pilotto", copy it, and paste it here.

No Anthropic key yet? Try it with $1 of free AI

For invited testers: paste the founder key you got with your invite. While you use the free credit, AI requests go through the Job Pilotto website to Anthropic (nothing is stored). With your own key they go straight from this Mac to Anthropic. When the $1 is used, add your own key in Settings → Anthropic.

Step 2 of 5

Your Job Pilotto workspace in Notion

Notion is where you'll see and track everything: your matches, applications, replies, interviews and insights. A free Notion account is enough.

  1. Click Connect with Notion below. Notion opens in your browser.
  2. Keep Use a template provided by the developer → Allow access. Notion adds a ready Job Pilotto page to your workspace.
  3. Come back here. Job Pilotto finishes setting it up, about a minute.

I already have a Job Pilotto page
  1. Click Connect with Notion above. On Notion's page choose Select pages → tick only your Job Pilotto page → Allow access.
  2. Job Pilotto uses what's there and adds anything missing. (An empty page named Job Pilotto works too: everything is built inside it.)
Having trouble? Use a token instead
  1. Make a page for it: in Notion's sidebar, + (new page) → name it Job Pilotto and leave it empty. Job Pilotto builds everything inside it when you connect. (Or copy → Duplicate; missing parts are added the same way.)
  2. Create a connection: open Notion's Developer tools → Connections → New connection → name it "Job Pilotto", keep API token → Create connection → copy the token (starts with ntn_).
  3. Give it access: on the connection's Content access tab → Edit access → under Private → Top level pages, tick only Job Pilotto → Save.
  4. Paste the token here.
